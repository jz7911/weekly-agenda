How to use it
Click any line to edit it. Type in the bottom line of a section and press Enter to add an item.
Age tag The number beside an item is how many weeks it has been carried. At 4 weeks it turns amber, at 8 it turns red. That is the signal to schedule it, delegate it, or kill it.
Star marks a commitment you made to someone else. W marks something you are waiting on from someone else, which is the one category most likely to go missing.
Start new week files everything checked off into Closed out this week, bumps the date forward seven days, and ages the rest.
Printer icon on any card prints that card alone, on its own page, which is how you hand a superintendent their sheet. The icon beside it splits a card into two lists if you ever want their agenda and yours side by side.
Waiting on items take the date you asked. The day count turns amber at a week and red at two.
Meeting cards have a ruled pad for notes and a follow ups list underneath. The pad clears when you start a new week and the week's notes are kept under Past weeks.
Monthly review does not age weekly and is not meant to be worked weekly. Mark it reviewed and it will tell you when a month has gone by.
